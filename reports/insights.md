# Key findings and recommendations

_Based on simulated transaction data for a real business. See README.md._

## Findings

1. **Growing business.** Revenue grew 25% in 2025 and is up 33% for January to August 2026 against the same months of 2025.
2. **Very seasonal.** June to September brings 70% of all revenue. Chilli seed must be sown from late April to be ready for the June rush.
3. **Chilli is the core.** Chilli is 65% of revenue; VNR 332 (Rani) from VNR Seeds is the single biggest variety. 12 of 16 varieties make 80% of revenue.
4. **Pro-tray is winning.** Chilli pro-tray share rose from 58% to 71%. Pro-tray chilli sells 43% higher and earns a 31% gross margin, against 12% for bed chilli, where lower germination wastes costly hybrid seed.
5. **Dealers matter.** Wholesale and dealer customers are 48% of revenue with an average order of ₹8,962, but 38% of their revenue is on credit.
6. **Loyal farmers.** 62% of 2024's new customers bought again in 2025.
7. **Tobacco is volume, not profit.** Tobacco 428 is the largest crop by seedling count but earns about ₹21 gross profit per 1,000 seedlings, against ₹475 for pro-tray chilli.
8. **Waste to cut.** About 10% of pro-tray seedlings raised go unsold, mostly from over-sowing as a buffer.
9. **Going digital.** WhatsApp orders reached 25% in 2026 and UPI rose from 33% to 50% of revenue.

## Recommendations

1. Book seed and cocopeat for chilli, capsicum and tomato by April, using the sowing plan in section 10.
2. Cut unsold pro-tray seedlings from about 10% to 5% by taking advance orders (with a deposit) on WhatsApp and sowing to order.
3. Push pro-tray for chilli and capsicum: higher price, higher germination and better margin per seedling.
4. Set a credit limit and a 30-day due date for dealers, and track outstanding amounts weekly.
5. Keep tobacco for customer loyalty, but price it per 1,000 with a small increase or bundle it with chilli orders.
6. Use the website and WhatsApp for pre-season booking, and ask satisfied farmers for Google reviews after each season.
